Decomposition of Ferrous Sulphate Crystals

Understanding Thermal Decomposition

Today, we will explore the fascinating process of decomposition reactions, focusing on what happens when ferrous sulphate crystals are heated.
Ferrous Sulphate Crystals
Color: Green
Chemical Formula: FeSO4.7H2O
Ferrous sulphate crystals, known as FeSO4.7H2O, are initially green in color. This is an important characteristic to note before we apply heat.
As we heat the ferrous sulphate crystals, we observe a change in color and the release of gases. This is a key part of the thermal decomposition process.

Products of Decomposition

Decomposition of Ferrous Sulphate
Ferric Oxide (Fe2O3)
Sulfur Dioxide (SO2)
Sulfur Trioxide (SO3)
The thermal decomposition of ferrous sulphate results in the formation of ferric oxide, sulfur dioxide, and sulfur trioxide. Let's explore these products.
