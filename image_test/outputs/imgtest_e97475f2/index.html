Soil erosion is the process of the soil cover being worn away and washed down by natural forces like water and wind. This process can disrupt the balance of soil formation and erosion.

Soil erosion can be caused by both human activities and natural forces. Human actions like deforestation and over-grazing significantly increase erosion rates, while natural forces like wind and water also play a crucial role.

Effects of Soil Erosion

The effects of soil erosion can be devastating. It leads to the formation of gullies and ravines, making land unfit for cultivation. This not only affects agriculture but also disrupts local ecosystems.
